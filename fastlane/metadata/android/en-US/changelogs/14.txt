Release 2.2.0+14

Feature:
* New Portrait Mode editor powered by on-device PP-HumanSeg segmentation: blur the background or remove it entirely from photos with people
* New Magic Eraser editor that removes unwanted objects: brush over an object, EfficientSAM segments it and LaMa inpainting fills the area so it looks like the object was never there
* New AI Image Detector that estimates how likely a photo is AI-generated with a confidence band (Likely AI-generated / Unclear / Likely camera-original) and a probability percentage

Changes:
* The new AI features download their models once on demand over the internet on first use
* Reduced and bounded ML memory usage for improved stability on low-memory devices
